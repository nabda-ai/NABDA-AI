03 / BATTERY INTELLIGENCE

Understand the battery.
Improve the insight.

We explore diagnostic methods and treatment concepts designed to help engineers understand battery condition and evaluate performance.

RESEARCH & PROTOTYPING

From electrical signals to meaningful analysis.

Battery behavior can be studied through voltage, current, temperature, and time-based response. These measurements can help guide testing and support more informed evaluation.

  • Battery diagnostics and condition monitoring
  • Sulfation research and pulse-treatment concepts
  • Sensor measurements and data analysis
  • Performance evaluation under defined test conditions

Recovery outcomes depend on battery chemistry, condition, and test protocol. Research concepts are not a guarantee of restoring every battery.

04 / HOW IT WORKS

From diagnosis to decision.

A six-step workflow concept for evaluating battery condition and deciding what to do next.

ILLUSTRATIVE CONCEPT
STEP 01 / 06

Establish the baseline

Measure voltage, current, and temperature to record the battery's starting condition.

STEP 02 / 06

Interpret the signals

Compare measurements against the battery type and test conditions to identify likely causes of degradation.

STEP 03 / 06

Apply a controlled treatment

For suitable lead-acid batteries, evaluate controlled pulse treatment as a research concept within defined limits.

STEP 04 / 06

Watch the response

Track the battery during treatment with real-time sensing to detect unexpected behavior.

STEP 05 / 06

Measure again

Repeat the baseline tests under the same conditions so before-and-after results can be compared fairly.

STEP 06 / 06

Make an informed decision

Use the results to decide whether to continue using the battery, repeat the process, or retire it.

Illustrative workflow concept. Outcomes depend on battery chemistry, condition, and a validated test protocol.
